# MediNear – Emergency Medical Assistance Platform

## Overview

MediNear is a healthcare emergency assistance platform designed to help users quickly locate and contact nearby medical services during critical situations.

During emergencies, every minute matters. MediNear provides instant access to nearby hospitals, pharmacies, clinics, ambulance services, emergency guidance, and personal emergency information through a simple and user-friendly interface.

---

## Problem Statement

In medical emergencies, people often face challenges such as:

* Finding nearby hospitals quickly
* Locating open pharmacies at night
* Accessing ambulance services
* Knowing what actions to take before medical help arrives
* Remembering important medical information during panic situations

These delays can significantly impact emergency response time.

---

## Solution

MediNear acts as a centralized emergency healthcare assistance platform that allows users to:

* Discover nearby healthcare facilities
* Access emergency services instantly
* Receive symptom-based guidance
* Store emergency medical information
* Contact healthcare providers with one tap

---

## Key Features

Locate nearby:

### Interactive Map Integration

### Smart Search & Filters

Search healthcare facilities and apply filters such as:

* Open Now
* 24 Hours

* Pharmacies

### One-Tap Calling

Instantly contact:

* Hospitals
* Pharmacies
* Clinics
* Ambulance services

Designed to reduce response time during emergencies.

---

### SOS Emergency Mode

A dedicated emergency interface providing:

* Call Ambulance
* Nearest Hospital
* Nearest Pharmacy
* Emergency Contacts
* Quick Directions

Simplified for high-stress emergency situations.

### Symptom-Based Emergency Guidance

Provides immediate guidance for common emergencies:

* Chest Pain
* High Fever
* Accident/Injury
* Breathing Difficulty

* Accident Assistance
